Balanced Meals for Everyday Energy

The Balanced Plate

A simple way to build healthy meals without counting calories.

½ Vegetables

Fill half your plate with colorful veggies for vitamins, fiber, and volume.

¼ Protein

Choose lean meats, tofu, beans, eggs, or fish to stay full longer.

¼ Whole Grains

Pick whole grains like brown rice, quinoa, oats, or whole‑grain pasta.

Healthy Fats

Add a small amount of nuts, seeds, avocado, or olive oil.
